Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

ClipIQ is a desktop tool that MegaClipping runs on its own computer to post its own clips to its own accounts. This page explains what ClipIQ accesses and how it handles it.

Who uses ClipIQ

Only MegaClipping. ClipIQ has no public sign-up and no user accounts, and it collects no information about viewers or other people.

What ClipIQ accesses

When MegaClipping connects one of its accounts, the platform grants ClipIQ limited access:

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos to the channel, and read the channel's name to show which channel is connected.
  • Instagram (Meta): read the account's basic profile (user name) and publish Reels to it.
  • TikTok: read the account's basic profile (display name) and upload and publish videos to it.

ClipIQ uses this access only to post clips that MegaClipping has approved, and to show which account is connected. It does not read, download or store anyone else's data.

How it is stored

The access tokens the platforms issue are stored only on MegaClipping's own computer, encrypted and locked to its Windows user account. They are sent only to the platform they belong to, when ClipIQ posts or renews the connection. There is no ClipIQ server, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

To publish a Reel, Instagram fetches the video from a link. For those few minutes, the clip is kept in MegaClipping's own storage bucket behind a link that expires after an hour, and it is deleted once Instagram has it.

Google user data

ClipIQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnecting an account in ClipIQ deletes its tokens from the computer.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time on the platform's side: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, in Instagram under Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, and in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
