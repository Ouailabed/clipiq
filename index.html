ClipIQ

A private clipping tool run by MegaClipping.

ClipIQ is a desktop tool that MegaClipping runs on its own computer. It finds the best moments in GTA roleplay streams, cuts them into short vertical clips with captions, and posts the clips MegaClipping approves to MegaClipping's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, through each platform's official API.

ClipIQ is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up for it or sign in to it, and it doesn't collect anything from viewers. Clips credit the streamer they come from.
