What ClipIQ is

ClipIQ is a desktop tool that MegaClipping runs for its own use, to cut short clips from gaming streams and post them to its own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Using the platforms

ClipIQ posts through each platform's official API and follows the terms of YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and their developer policies. Every clip is reviewed and approved by MegaClipping before it is posted.
